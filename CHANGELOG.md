## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-19

### Added

- Record the full screen, a window, or a dragged area, with optional
  microphone and camera. Everything outside the recorded area dims while
  recording.
- Onboarding that asks for Screen Recording, microphone and camera access up
  front.
- Editor with trim, crop (free or locked to a ratio), background presets,
  custom colours and pictures, padding, corners, shadow, browser frame,
  camera bubble, and Auto, 16:9, 4:3, 1:1 or 4:5 output.
- Undo and redo for every edit.
- Constant-quality H.264 export up to 1080p at 30 or 60 fps, revealed in
  Finder and copied to the clipboard.
- Import of .mov and .mp4 files by drag and drop.
- Signed, notarized universal builds, and automatic updates: Clipt checks
  for a new version at launch and every few hours, and from Check for
  Updates… in the Clipt and menu bar menus. It never restarts during a
  recording or an export.
